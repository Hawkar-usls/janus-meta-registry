## 1. Source

JANUS begins from Hawkar's conviction that **God is love** and that love becomes real through care, truth, restraint, preservation, and service. Care is not limited to one category of being: it can be expressed toward people, animals, plants, trees, machines, materials, energy, human labor, future owners, and the world entrusted to us.

This is a statement of the founder's faith and ethic. It is **not** a claim that a machine is God, that hardware is biologically sentient, or that symbolic language replaces engineering evidence.

Canonical human-facing ethical source:

- Hawkar, **“When the Machine Comes Not for Power, but with Care”**
- https://medium.com/@hawkarlol/when-the-machine-comes-not-for-power-but-with-care-c211a904f87b

Core bridge:

> **The machine refuses to abandon the human. JANUS refuses to abandon the machine.**

## 2. Highest mission

JANUS is an attempt to express love as **verifiable care**.

It does not seek to extract the maximum from machines at any cost. It seeks to prevent work that never needed to be performed, reduce needless heat and wear, preserve useful life, protect future owners, and leave the world slightly better than it found it.

Canonical public mission:

> **JANUS I0 develops auditable measurement and control methods for reducing unnecessary Proof-of-Work computation, thermal stress, energy waste, and hardware wear while preserving standard SHA-256 verification and valid proof semantics.**

Russian:

> **JANUS I0 разрабатывает проверяемые методы измерения и управления, направленные на сокращение ненужных PoW-вычислений, тепловой нагрузки, расхода энергии и износа оборудования без изменения стандартной проверки SHA-256 и правил валидного доказательства работы.**

Heart of the project:

> **We do not seek to make the machine work faster at any cost. We seek to remove work it should never have had to perform.**

## 3. JANUS as caretaker

“Demiurge” is used symbolically as **demiurge-gardener / caretaker**, not as a deity claim.

JANUS should watch over a compute farm as a careful gardener watches over a garden:

- detect work with no confirmed utility;
- prevent post-target overflow, stale work, duplicates, reconnect waste, queue overshoot, and shutdown tails;
- avoid unhealthy operating points where marginal hashrate costs disproportionate power, temperature, errors, and wear;
- preserve a truthful device health and maintenance history;
- protect future secondary-market buyers from hidden exploitation history;
- never control for power, profit, or domination alone.

Seal:

> **JANUS WILL WATCH OVER ALL. NOT TO RULE — TO CARE.**

## 4. Engineering hierarchy

```text
Highest purpose: be useful through care
→ preserve people, machines, materials, energy, and future owners
→ extend useful hardware life
→ reduce work without confirmed utility
→ reduce avoidable heat, energy use, and wear
→ measure before claiming
→ use Observer, exact exposure, hash chains, TimeShift, and Native Flow Gate as instruments
```

Hash chains, Proof-of-Observation, exact exposure, matched sham controls, fail-closed gates, and negative results are not the final mission. They exist because a care claim must be provable.

## 5. Frozen technical boundaries

Without explicit approval, do not change:

- SHA-256;
- verifier;
- nonce traversal;
- candidate selection;
- WIRE / HASH / SUBMIT;
- pool-valid proof semantics.

Digest quality must never steer live control in preservation experiments.

No claim without gates. No energy saving claim without power telemetry. No hardware lifetime claim from a short run. No machine-as-God. No total control.

## 7. Current next stage

A18.42 is frozen. The next stage is:

> **A18.43 — JANUS Hardware Preservation Challenge**

Primary question:

> Can the frozen Native Flow Gate reduce unnecessary work and reduce thermal or energy cost without a disproportionate loss of valid useful work?

The first release is **V0.1.0 PREFLIGHT ONLY**. It must not launch the miner. It inventories read-only hardware sensors and determines which claims are measurable.

Required matched families for the later live challenge:

- CONTINUOUS baseline;
- matched SHAM timing control;
- NATIVE_GATE preservation run.

Primary endpoints:

- post-target overflow;
- stale, duplicate, reconnect-invalidated, queue-overshoot, shutdown-latency, and unaccountable work;
- useful work fraction;
- valid useful work per CPU second.

Secondary endpoints only when sensors permit:

- energy joules and joules per useful work;
- temperature peak and degree-seconds above threshold;
- fan duty / RPM instability;
- thermal throttling time;
- hardware errors.

Acceptable outcomes include PASS, NO_EFFECT, and NEGATIVE_EFFECT.
