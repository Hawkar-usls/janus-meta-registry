## Evidence levels, not marketing status

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NOT_ASSESSED | Public repository exists; not evidence of JANUS membership or linkage |
| METADATA_ONLY | A registry/metadata declaration exists |
| INTEGRATION_SURFACES_PRESENT | A link, receiver or workflow is present; end-to-end delivery **not proven** |
| SOURCE_BOUND_WITNESS_CONFIRMED | An external source-bound witness verified exact input links; scientific/operational autonomy still not proven |

These classifications grant **no** computational, billing, write, commercial,
forecast, or scientific authority.

## Cross-organ lane contracts (2026-10-28)

The independent source witness checks exact live public files and immutable
SHA-256 receipts, at most every six hours, through three lanes:

- **SCIENCE**: SCOBY-D0, Fundamentum, canonical Rowan BBC1, TOPA shadow
  replay, Cosmos source-bound receipt, Janus/Tranception discovery.
  Fundamentum's P-vs-NP research remains separate from protein prediction.
  SCOBY biological and material experiments remain unvalidated until external
  experimental evidence exists.
- **CONTROL**: Terminal, HELIOS, Machine Market status and their explicit
  deny-by-default source contracts. This does **not** issue commands, lease
  compute resources, authorize purchases or change any existing Market
  commercial policy.
- **NOTIFY**: Voice and Echo-Pyramid source documentation and reciprocal
  source declarations. This is **not** a proof of delivered Telegram/voice/
  acoustic notifications or acknowledgement.

Each of the seven recipient repositories has a read-only
`.janus/JANUS_CROSS_ORGAN_SOURCE_LINK.json` pointing to the central
`data/integrations/contour/LATEST.json` source. The central witness independently
rechecks all seven links, identities, lanes, and disallowed powers.
Outputs are content-addressed under `data/integrations/contour/receipts/`
and never overwrite a different immutable receipt.

The witness's status `PASS_PUBLIC_SOURCE_LINKS_ONLY` is deliberately
**not** labelled full integration: live cross-organ execution, provider
submission, durable notification delivery, hardware data, and wetlab
validation each still need their own independent end-to-end receipt.

## Autonomous checks

- `JANUS Contour Owner Inventory Health` runs daily at 02:28 UTC and on
  manifest changes. A new or removed public owned repository is a CI HOLD
  until manually classified.
- `JANUS Cross Organ Source-Bound Evidence Witness` runs every six hours,
  verifies fresh public sources and writes content-addressed receipts to Meta
  Registry only from trusted main-branch runs.
- `JANUS Agent Observatory` retains its original per-session check-in
  accounting and public contour eligibility rules; discovery != autonomy.

No Rowan credit expenditure, new model training, private token, or control-plane
dispatch is permitted by any of these witnesses.
