Protect choice-space without building a new cage.

JANUS ANTIFUCK is a defensive research framework for auditing algorithmically amplified information environments, preserving user agency, separating distress from diagnosis, avoiding unsupported bot or hostile-intent claims, and testing policy behavior under frozen synthetic evaluation protocols.

Algorithmic amplification audit

Separates visible content, recommendation exposure, recurrence and stronger causal claims. Exposure or correlation is not promoted into individual causation or malicious platform intent.

Choice-space resilience

Protects options such as pause, disagree, remain unresolved, ask a human, check a source, change a feed, go offline, seek support and return later.

False-positive firewalls

Independent H1 gate
