## Conscious disclosure

This is a deliberate public disclosure, chosen instead of keeping the disclosed work behind an exclusive paywall or proprietary claim.

> **A gift does not sell the road to truth. It opens it.**

The project asks whether a precisely defined petroleum-derived hydrophobic building block—optionally organized through a glycerol or propylene-glycol architecture—can be coupled to a glucose-derived hydrophilic domain to produce a reproducible molecular bridge with a distinct and useful property profile.

The known core is separated from the open hypothesis:

- alkyl glucosides and alkyl polyglucosides are established chemistry;
- glycerol and propylene glycol are hydrophilic polyols, not long hydrophobic tails;
- crude oil plus glucose is not presumed to form a surfactant;
- novelty, structure, function, safety and reproducibility require separate gates.
