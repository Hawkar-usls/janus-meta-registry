## Blind gate

The candidate packet set hides NP identifiers, artifact titles and prior interpretations. A second ontology-blind representation removes project-native vocabulary. Reviewers should receive only these neutral packets for structural scoring.

## Matched-null gate

Nulls must be matched by artifact class, epoch, text density and relevant token density. Clock candidates must be compared with artifacts that also contain times. Mythopoetic candidates must be compared with similarly symbolic material.

## Claim ceiling

Even if a candidate exceeds matched nulls after blinding, that would establish only a structural excess under the frozen procedure. It would not establish prophecy, precognition, physical retrocausality, divine scheduling, supernatural causation or hidden authorial intent.
