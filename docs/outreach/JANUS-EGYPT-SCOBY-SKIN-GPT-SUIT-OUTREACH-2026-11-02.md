## Brief summary (English)
An Egypt-inspired speculative day/night wearable concept combining the visual texture of dried bacterial cellulose (SCOBY bio-leather) with the articulated structure of 3D-printed PLA chainmail fabric. In research, this would be a platform to test how bacterial cellulose grows around mobile links; whether the resulting composite can retain flexibility while improving specific material properties; and whether stabilized specimens can support low-cost tactile and thermal sensing through an experimental SkinGPT/ESP32-S3 data path.

**What is established:** the design and synthetic uncalibrated scenarios have been recorded; the SkinGPT software exists as a prototype. **What is not established:** a grown articulated composite, functional complete costume, durability or protection in use, ballistic/fragment resistance, self-healing, or field-tested sensing performance. Previous 346 MPa estimates apply to a different historical BC/hemp hypothetical laminate and are not transferable to this suit.
