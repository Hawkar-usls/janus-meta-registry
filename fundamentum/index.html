Carry the proof state, not just the conclusion.

Janus-Fundamentum is a proof-carrying computational research program. It preserves finite certificates, generators, transport steps, tamper canaries, open gates and negative results so a reader can distinguish what has been constructively checked from what remains mathematically unproved.

Deterministic certificate layers

Finite searches and constructive candidates are accompanied by machine-checkable artifacts, counts and tamper canaries instead of being summarized only in prose.

Open gates stay open

A successful finite family does not automatically generalize to arbitrary size. Missing transport or induction machinery remains explicitly OPEN rather than being inferred from a trend.

generalizationinductionclaim ceiling

Forbidden shortcuts are recorded

Proof routes that would collapse distinctions or rely on an invalid expand/join/shrink shortcut are marked as forbidden, preserving why the route cannot currently establish a global theorem.

External mathematics review
