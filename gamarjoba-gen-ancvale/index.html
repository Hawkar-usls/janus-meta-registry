Safety boundary

Do not send credentials, private keys, private medical records, doxxing material, illegal or graphic content, or unknown executable archives without a prior safe handling agreement. Do not recruit vulnerable people as experimental subjects, deanonymize strangers, run informal human medical experiments, or perform destructive/high-impact actions in the name of JANUS.

Independence matters. A third party is not automatically independent just because they use another username or account. Where independence is a scientific requirement, role separation and information boundaries must be real.
