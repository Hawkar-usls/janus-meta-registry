# TOCH method note v0.1

TOCH does **not** classify people as sane/insane, scientific/unscientific, or worthy/unworthy. It classifies **work products** by explicit methodological properties:

- Is the computational model explicit?
- Are parameters and quantifiers frozen?
- Is asymptotic cost stated?
- Is the claim falsifiable?
- Is there a verifier/certificate/witness relation?
- Is representation size and construction cost charged?
- Is there an independent attack or known counterfamily?
- Is the result a theorem, restricted theorem, conjecture, analogy, or failed resolution claim?

A predecessor is considered a strong signal for JANUS when a mechanism independently anticipates one of JANUS's current exact debts (certificate discovery, exact contraction, algebra discovery, extension-size accounting, adversarial barriers) **and** the original work exposes itself to mathematical refutation.
