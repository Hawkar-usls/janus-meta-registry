# JANUS Osiris Phallus Search Protocol v0.1

## Purpose

This lane does not assume that a surviving biological relic exists. It searches the evidence network behind the Osirian phallus tradition: Egyptian texts, local cult claims, ritual relic-simulacra, reliquary vessels, fish symbolism, Osirian figurines, funerary replacement practices and excavated contexts.

## Highest-priority leads

1. **Papyrus Brooklyn 47.218.84** — acquire direct catalogue/edition receipt for the Mendesian tradition linking the Osirian virile member with the spine/back and the 'copulating Ba'.
2. **Dendera roof chapels / Khoiak inscriptions** — extract the primary passages that assign phallus relic-simulacra to nomes, vessels and processional sequences.
3. **Mendes / Djedet / Tell el-Ruba** — resolve excavation and temple-sector evidence around Banebdjedet and the Osirian component of the local theology.
4. **Oxyrhynchos** — keep fish-receptacle mythology separate from the archaeological Osirian-figurine catacomb context.
5. **Kenem / Upper Egyptian nome 7 tradition** — resolve the ancient toponym to a secure modern archaeological location before making any site claim.

## Hard controls

- Min iconography is not Osiris by default.
- A generic phallic amulet is not the phallus of Osiris.
- A Mendes fish-standard is not automatically one of Plutarch's phallus-consuming fish.
- A Khoiak 'finding' episode is a ritual event, not archaeological coordinates.
- The Kem/Three Kings story is a deliberately separate creative branch and never supplies historical evidence.

## Promotion rule

A candidate object may move beyond `SIMULACRUM_OR_RITUAL_OBJECT` only with secure archaeological provenance, inscriptional identification or equivalent primary documentation, and independent material/publication support. Until then the biological-relic claim remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
