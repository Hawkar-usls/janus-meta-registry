# Osiris Phallus research lane

The lane starts from a strict distinction between mythic loss, ritual reconstruction, local relic claims, archaeological objects and modern creative retellings.

Current source-audited state:

- Plutarch preserves a late variant in which the organ is not found because fish consume it and Isis consecrates a replica.
- Egyptian sources independently associate the phallus with Osiris and stage repeated body-part recovery/reconstitution in Khoiak rites.
- Late Period/Ptolemaic-Roman evidence preserves local relic-simulacrum networks rather than one universally fixed physical object.
- Mendes/Djedet is the strongest current textual-geographic lead for a phallus-plus-spine/back theology and must be pursued through Papyrus Brooklyn 47.218.84 and excavation/temple evidence.
- No authenticated surviving biological relic is currently established.

The creative Kem/Three Kings universe is archived separately and may inspire search questions but cannot raise evidence confidence.
