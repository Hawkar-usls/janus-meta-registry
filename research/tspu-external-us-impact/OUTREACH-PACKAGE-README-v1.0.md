1. **Confirmed:** Russian/Russian-backed conflict operations repeatedly neutralized, seized, replaced or rerouted information-distribution infrastructure.
2. **Confirmed:** TSPU is widely deployed, in-path and centrally controlled inside Russia.
3. **Confirmed:** Russia has demonstrated selective protocol censorship, regional/mobile shutdowns and deny-except-allowlist operation.
4. **Confirmed:** Russian SORM/DPI technology is exported to multiple foreign telecom environments.
5. **Confirmed:** Russian malign influence operations target U.S. and foreign audiences.
6. **Risk assessment:** Russian-component foreign surveillance systems may present vendor/Russian access risk; degree of access is unclear.
7. **Not established:** Roskomnadzor-operated TSPU nodes in sovereign third countries.
8. **Not established:** Russian state remote command of sovereign foreign SORM/DPI deployments.
9. **Not established:** Synchronized multi-country TSPU-like activation.
