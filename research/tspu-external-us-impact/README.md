# TSPU External / US Impact Observatory

Status: `ACTIVE_DEFENSIVE_THREAT_INTELLIGENCE`

## Mission

This branch tracks evidence that Russian TSPU-related censorship capabilities, adjacent Russian DPI/SORM technology, or Russian-controlled network paths may create effects outside the Russian domestic Internet. Its defensive goal is to forecast activation events early, distinguish real external effects from coincidence, estimate consequences for United States networks/users/services, and preserve falsifiers as carefully as confirming evidence.

## Claim firewall

1. TSPU, SORM, and commercial DPI are related but distinct systems. Never relabel one as another without device/operator evidence.
2. Export of Russian surveillance technology does not prove remote Russian state control.
3. A service outage is not evidence of TSPU unless route/path and packet-level fingerprints are consistent.
4. Direct impact on a foreign network requires a foreign-vantage measurement or verified route through a controlled segment.
5. GitHub/OpenAI provider RCAs outrank speculative network explanations unless an independent network-level bridge is demonstrated.
6. Forecasts are defensive hypotheses, not accusations.

## Core distinction

`domestic censorship` → demonstrated.

`Russian-side cross-border traffic control` → demonstrated.

`Russian DPI/SORM export abroad` → demonstrated as an adjacent ecosystem.

`Roskomnadzor-operated TSPU outside Russia` → not established.

`direct TSPU censorship of non-Russian-origin users outside Russia` → not established.

The branch exists to test the last two claims rather than assume them.
